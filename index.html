How it works

  1. Pick your dating method. Most people use the first day of their last period (LMP); if you know your conception date instead, use that toggle.
  2. Enter the date. That's the only input needed.
  3. We add 280 days. This is the standard "Naegele's rule" estimate — 40 weeks from LMP, or 266 days from conception (accounting for the ~2 weeks before ovulation).
  4. See your progress. Your current gestational age, trimester, and days remaining are calculated from today's date automatically.
  5. Track milestones. Key dates like end of first trimester, viability, and full term are laid out on a timeline, with passed ones marked off.

This is a statistical estimate, not a prediction — only about 5% of babies arrive exactly on their due date. Your OB/midwife's ultrasound-based dating is generally more precise.
